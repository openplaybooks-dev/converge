# White-Space Map

## Crowded zones to avoid

The market is dense with literal AI-agent and workflow language. Avoid names built around `Agent*`, `AI*`, `Lang*`, `Chain*`, `Crew*`, `Flow*`, `Graph*`, `Task*`, `Auto*`, `Bot*`, `Forge*`, `Ops*`, `Run*`, and `Check*`. These patterns blur into AI orchestration tools, workflow engines, task runners, CI systems, and agent frameworks. Also avoid Copilot-like sidekick names, generic Latin SaaS constructions, and random five-letter vowel names that sound premium but carry little meaning.

## Opportunity zones to explore

1. **Proof and verification artifacts** — proof, attestation, manifest, seal, receipt, certificate, evidence.
2. **Weaving, binding, stitching, edges, seams** — names that imply durable coordination and careful joining.
3. **Navigation instruments and bearings** — compass, bearing, chart, waypoint, course, sextant-like precision.
4. **Musical structure and counterpoint** — score, cadence, measure, canon, harmony, conductor metaphors.
5. **Publishing proofs and galleys** — reviewable artifacts, editorial marks, editions, press-ready confidence.
6. **Graph theory without saying graph** — lattice, mesh, path, span, join, adjacency, cut, invariant.
7. **Craft precision and fit** — gauge, caliper, tolerance, joinery, fit, finish, true edges.
8. **State machines, ledgers, traces, receipts** — transition records and accountable execution history.
9. **Ecological networks and mycelial systems** — distributed coordination with organic resilience.
10. **Mathematical convergence and fixed points** — closure, limits, attractors, invariants, fixed points.
11. **Ritual/checkpoint/completion language** — signoff, pass, closure, rites, done-state confidence.
12. **Calm control and enterprise trust** — composed names that feel dependable without enterprise bloat.

## Naming thesis

The strongest white space is not another AI agent, flow, chain, or graph. It is the language of trustworthy completion: systems that bind many moving parts into an inspectable artifact, leave evidence behind, and guide teams toward convergence.
