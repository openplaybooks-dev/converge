Q2 Team Capacity Recovery Plan

Two senior engineers are transitioning off the mobile redesign project, creating a 20% capacity gap. A three-week recovery plan redistributes critical features to available senior staff, defers non-critical scope by two weeks, and avoids contractor overhead.

Status: Pending Review Updated: May 23, 2026, 7:45 PM Playbook: handoff-review
Executive summary
Team is one sprint behind on the mobile redesign. The gap is recoverable without adding headcount, but non-critical path items must be deprioritized for three weeks. No blockers on core release; API integration is on track.
Recommendation
Approve scope shift: defer the notification preferences screen and bulk-import feature. These are valuable but non-blocking. Re-evaluate at the June 1 checkpoint. Core analytics dashboard and auth fixes must ship by June 6.
Decision requested
Confirm the deferred scope list. No action needed on staffing—the team is aligned and burn rate is acceptable within current budget headroom.
Tradeoffs
Speed vs. quality: rushing the deferred features later introduces regression risk. Scope vs. timeline: keeping full scope and extending by four weeks delays Q2 OKR measurement. Internal vs. external: contractor support rejected due to onboarding overhead outweighing short-term output gain.
What to check
  • Is the deferred scope list correct—would you keep or restore any of it?
