# Concept A: The Playbook — Image Generation Prompt

## Prompt

```
A minimal, iconic logo mark for an AI developer tool called "Converge". The mark is a simple geometric playbook (book) icon. An open book viewed from above, the spine at the bottom, pages fanning upward. The left and right pages angle inward, their inner edges converging at the spine to form a subtle checkmark shape in the negative space. Flat vector style. Clean geometric lines. Dark indigo background (#0F172A). The mark itself in a single light color (white or light violet #C4B5FD). No gradients, no shadows, pure flat vector icon. The style should feel like a premium developer tool — Stripe, Vercel, Linear quality. Symmetrical. Centered. The mark should work as a favicon at 16x16. No text in the image.
```

## Design notes

- The "book" + "checkmark" double read is the key
- Should work as a single filled silhouette
- The checkmark is formed by the convergence of the pages at the spine
- Simple enough to be recognizable at 16×16
