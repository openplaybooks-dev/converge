# Playbook Concept — Polished Variants

## P1 — Abstract Geometric Book

```
A ultra-minimal logo mark. A book formed from exactly 3 geometric shapes: two identical angled trapezoids (the left and right pages) leaning inward, meeting at a thin vertical center line (the spine). The two page-shapes angle slightly outward at the top, creating a subtle V-shaped opening. The convergence of the pages toward the spine forms an arrow or checkmark reading. Pure flat vector. Single white color on dark indigo #0F172A background. No gradients, no shadows, no rounded corners — sharp geometric edges. Think: Stripe-level minimalism. Centered. Icon-only, no text.
```

## P2 — Bookmark = Checkmark

```
A clever logo mark with a double meaning. A minimal book shape (a simple rectangle with a spine on the left side) viewed from the front. A bookmark ribbon hangs from the top of the book, running down the cover. The ribbon's tail curls into a checkmark shape at the bottom right. The checkmark IS the bookmark. The bookmark IS verification. Flat vector. Single white/light-violet color on dark indigo #0F172A background. No gradients, no shadows. Clean, clever, iconic. The "aha" should come from realizing the bookmark forms a check. No text.
```

## P3 — Binder Rings

```
A technical, industrial logo mark. Three evenly-spaced binder rings (small perfect circles) on the left side, with pages fanning outward to the right in a radial pattern. The pages are simple clean lines of varying lengths, like code lines or sheet music. The three rings are the convergence points that hold everything together. Flat vector style. Single white color on dark indigo #0F172A background. Sharp, precise, geometric. Feels like a blueprint or schematic. No text.
```

## P4 — Code-Page Book

```
A logo mark that merges a book with code. A minimal open book viewed from above, but instead of solid pages, the pages are rendered as thin parallel horizontal lines (like lines of code or terminal output). The left and right halves angle inward symmetrically toward a central spine. The spine is a bright solid vertical line. The overall silhouette reads as an open book, but zoom in and it's code. Flat vector. Single light-green #34D399 color on dark indigo #0F172A. Developer-native feel. No text.
```

## P5 — Single Folded Page

```
A minimal logo mark: a single square page folded diagonally from top-right corner to bottom-left corner. The folded corner creates a triangle shape. The diagonal fold crease line is sharp and prominent. The shape reads as both a page being folded AND a convergence arrow. Ultra-minimal: essentially one rotated square with one diagonal line cutting through it. Flat vector. Single white color on dark indigo 0F172A background. Maximum minimalism, Stripe level simplicity. No text at all.
```
