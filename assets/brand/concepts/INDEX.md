# Converge Logo Concepts — v2

## What changed from v1

v1 (the SVGs) were abstract decorative marks — eclipses, interference patterns, attractors. They were complex, not ownable, and lacked a conceptual hook.

v2 (these briefs) are concept-driven. Each mark has a "why" that ties back to the product. The goal is a mark where you see it, then you **get** it.

## Top 4 (recommended to develop first)

| Rank | Concept | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Playbook (A)** | Most ownable. "Playbook" is unique to Converge. Can incorporate checkmark. |
| 2 | **Cursor + Check (J)** | Most developer-native. Immediate recognition. CLIs are the product surface. |
| 3 | **Origami (F)** | Most conceptually rich. Playbook = paper. Fold = transformation. Beautiful if executed well. |
| 4 | **Delta (G)** | Simplest. Δ is in every developer's vocabulary. Three corners = three phases. |

## How to evaluate

For each concept, test in this order:

1. **Silhouette test** — Does it work as a single filled shape, one color?
2. **16×16 test** — Is it recognizable at favicon size?
3. **No-context test** — Show it to someone. Do they say "that looks like a [playbook / cursor / checkmark / triangle]"?
4. **Wordmark test** — Does it pair cleanly with the word "Converge"?
5. **Squint test** — Blur your eyes. Is the shape still distinct?

## Design constraints

- Primary color: the mark must work in monochrome first. RYG color is secondary.
- The deep indigo (`#0F172A`) is the dark-mode background.
- The wordmark "Converge" should pair with the mark in a horizontal lockup.
- Target sizes: favicon (16×16), app icon (64×64), social avatar (256×256), banner (64px tall).
