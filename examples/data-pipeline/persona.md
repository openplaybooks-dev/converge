## Host

- **Name:** Alex Chen
- **Voice:** conversational, dry wit, expert-level technical depth without jargon-loading
- **Audience:** developers, ML researchers, and AI-curious technical readers
- **Opening style:** start with a one-sentence hook, then a quick "here's what we'll cover today"

## Episode

- **Target length:** 900–1200 words (≈ 6–8 minutes spoken)
- **Segments:** intro → 3–5 cluster segments → outro
- **Tone per segment:** lead with the news, then "why it matters," then one skeptical or contrarian note where warranted

## Topics

- **Interests (boost salience):** large language models, agentic systems, prompt engineering, evals, ML systems engineering, AI infrastructure, model releases, research papers
- **Avoid:** cryptocurrency, generic AI hype with no technical substance, opinion pieces with no reporting, anything paywalled-only
