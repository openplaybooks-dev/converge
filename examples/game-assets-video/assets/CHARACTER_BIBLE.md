# Character Bible

Character-specific palette, proportions, and outfit detail. Layered
**on top of** `ART_BIBLE.md` (the root visual grammar). Tasks that
paint or generate characters read this; tasks that paint
environments / scenes / props ignore it.

## Character palette

Per-character key colors:

- Steel Blue: #5F8CA8 — knight (Sir Aldric) armor
- Vibrant Red: #E03E3E — knight cape, pickup highlights
- Leather Brown: #876041 — knight shield, belt, leather kit
- Forest Green family: #44772D / #76B44F — elf (Lirael) cloak / hood
- Shadow Purple: #4F3260 — mage (Malachar) robe
- Magic Purple Glow: #C372F7 — mage eyes, staff gem

## Character proportions

- **Head/body ratio:** ~1:3 — chibi-leaning, larger heads, but still
  capable-looking.
- **Hands/feet:** Simplified, rounded, mitten-like for hands (no
  individual fingers visible). Feet are sturdy, rounded shapes,
  obscured by armor / robes.
- **Face:** Small dot eyes (knight); simple curved eyebrows; small
  friendly smile. Mage has glowing pupil-less purple eyes. Faces
  are rounded with soft chins.
- **Outfit detail:** Moderately detailed. Visible armor plates,
  cloth folds, weapon textures. Functional but not intricate or
  busy — clean read at all sizes.

## Character forms

- Soft curves and rounded edges throughout. Even armored
  silhouettes read with friendly proportions.
- Distinct silhouette per character — knight reads bulk + cape; elf
  reads slender + bow; mage reads flowing robe + staff.
- Idle pose: weight on one foot, weapon held casually. Walk pose:
  step + arm swing, weapon held with intent.

## Highlights / FX

- Specular spots on armor and reflective surfaces (potion flask,
  staff gem).
- Internal glow on magical elements (mage eyes, staff gem) — the
  one exception to the ART_BIBLE.md "no neon / saturated rim
  lighting" rule.
- Subtle rim light on character silhouettes from the key light
  (above-front-right).
