# play-terrain

Static collision/visual terrain on the play parallax layer.
Decorations (rocks, flowers, bushes, thorn-patches) replace ground at
their cells — they're not stacked. Multi-row tokens (ground-bumpy,
pond, platforms) span their full footprint.
