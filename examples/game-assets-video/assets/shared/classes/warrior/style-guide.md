# warrior Class Style Guide

## Overview
The warrior class embodies grounded strength and frontline resolve. Visually defined by heavy plate armor, a melee weapon held in firm grip, and a wide, weight-bearing stance that reads as immovable at rest and decisive in motion. Silhouettes are broad and blocky — wide pauldrons, thick gauntlets, planted boots — never lean. Designs read as steadfast and protective at first glance, contrasting the flowing drape of mages and the alert poise of rangers. Hand-drawn 2D in the project's painterly style: soft shading, clean rounded silhouettes, warm storybook lighting with subtle rim light along the pauldrons and weapon edge.

## Color Palette
- **Primary**: Steel grey (#7A828C), gunmetal (#4A5058) — plate armor body, helm, greaves.
- **Secondary**: Deep crimson (#8C2A2A), oxblood (#5A1818) — surcoat, tabard, cloth padding visible at joints.
- **Accent (metallic)**: Polished silver (#C8CCD0), warm brass (#B8893A) — weapon edge, buckles, pauldron trim, crest.
- **Neutrals**: Warm leather brown (#5A3820), bone white (#E8DCC8) — belt, grip wrap, undertunic at collar.
- Constraint: 16-color palette per project rules. Metallic highlights are reserved for weapon edge and trim; the surcoat crimson is the only saturated chromatic note.

## Materials
- **Armor type**: heavy — full plate over mail and gambeson. Articulated pauldrons, breastplate, vambraces, greaves, sabatons. Metal reads as forged and lightly weathered, with painterly highlights along ridges.
- **Clothing**: crimson surcoat or tabard worn over the breastplate, belted at the waist; padded gambeson visible at the neckline and behind the knees. Leather belt with buckled fastenings.
- **Weapon style**: melee — one-handed sword (broadsword or arming sword) carried at rest in the dominant hand, point angled toward the ground. Optional kite or heater shield in the off-hand. Blade length roughly two-thirds character height.
- **Trinkets**: pommel emblem, brass buckles, a small clan crest etched into the pauldron or shield face.

## Design Elements
- **Helm**: closed or open-faced great-helm with a narrow visor slit; reads as the silhouette's apex. Optional plume or crest in muted brass.
- **Pauldrons**: oversized, the widest point of the silhouette — signature class identifier. Always rendered prominently, catching the strongest rim light.
- **Surcoat hem**: terminates mid-thigh with a clean, square-cut edge; sways minimally to read as heavy fabric.
- **Sword posture**: held vertically or angled down at rest in the dominant hand; weapon remains readable in silhouette at all times.
- **Planted stance**: weight distributed evenly across both feet, shoulder-width apart — never on the ball of the foot. Reads as ready to hold ground, not to dart.
- **Symmetry**: both pauldrons matched, both gauntlets matched; asymmetry only via the surcoat crest or off-hand shield.

## Animation Style
- **Idle**: minimal vertical motion (1 px breath cycle). Surcoat hem barely moves; plume drifts faintly. Sword held steady; subtle helm tilt suggests watchfulness without breaking the planted read.
- **Walk**: heavy, deliberate stride — heel contacts ground first, full foot plants before the next step. Pauldrons rock slightly with each step. Sword held steady in dominant hand, off-hand swings only modestly. Suggestion of mass and inertia, never lightness.
- **Pose style**: low center of gravity, square shoulders, vertical spine. Avoids crouches and flowing curves. Movements emphasize weight transfer and grounded plants over limb articulation or cloth physics.
- **Frame counts**: 4 frames idle, 8 frames walk (per project tech spec).

## Characters in Class
- hero-knight — steel plate with crimson surcoat, closed helm, arming sword and kite shield; the canonical exemplar of the class.
