# warrior Class Style Guide

## Overview

The warrior class embodies strength, valor, and martial prowess. Characters in this class project an aura of physical power and combat readiness, with heavy armor and melee weapons. The visual identity emphasizes durability, heroic presence, and disciplined combat technique.

## Color Palette

**Primary Colors:**
- Steel Gray (#708090) - Armor, metallic surfaces
- Dark Bronze (#8B4513) - Leather, aged metal
- Deep Red (#8B0000) - Accents, cloth elements

**Secondary Colors:**
- Iron (#434343) - Shadows, dark metal
- Brass (#B5A642) - Trim, decorative elements
- Crimson (#DC143C) - Cape, cloth details

**Accent Colors:**
- Silver (#C0C0C0) - Blade edges, highlights
- Gold (#FFD700) - **Use sparingly.** Reserved for a single small heraldic emblem or rank insignia, never armor plates, pauldrons, trim, weapon body, or grip wrap. Target ≤5% of sprite pixels.

## Materials

**Armor Type:** Heavy
- Plate armor with layered protection
- Chainmail underlayers
- Reinforced joints and pauldrons
- Battle-worn, practical design

**Clothing:**
- Leather straps and belts
- Heavy cloth surcoats or tabards
- Functional, minimal fabric
- Cape or cloak optional

**Weapon Materials:**
- Steel blades and metal weapons
- Leather-wrapped grips
- Metal guards and pommels
- Battle-tested, functional design

## Design Elements

**Common Visual Elements:**
- Heavy plate armor coverage
- Large shoulder pauldrons
- Melee weapons (swords, axes, maces)
- Shield or secondary weapon
- Battle scars and wear marks
- Heraldic symbols or emblems
- Sturdy, grounded stance

**Silhouette:**
- Broad shoulders and chest
- Strong, imposing presence
- Weapon extends reach
- Solid, rectangular proportions
- Heavier build than other classes

## Animation Style

**Movement Characteristics:**
- Strong, deliberate motion
- Weight and momentum emphasized
- Heavy footfalls
- Armor clanking and shifting
- Powerful, grounded movements

**Pose Style:**
- Wide, stable stance
- Weapon ready position
- Shield up or weapon raised
- Weight forward for aggression
- Confident, heroic posture

**Combat Style:**
- Close-quarters melee combat
- Powerful swings and strikes
- Shield blocks and parries
- Charging and rushing attacks
- Tank role, absorbing damage
