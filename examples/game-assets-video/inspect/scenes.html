How it works

Reads assets/scenes.json and the master atlas. For each scene, this view lays down the parallax bg layers, builds a ground from the scene tilesheet (frame 0 = first variant in the manifest), and stamps every scene_props spritesheet at ground level. Missing layers fall back to a flat sky and rectangles so partial scenes still preview.
