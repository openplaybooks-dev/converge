# Dreamwave Arcade

Build a playful React web app for discovering tiny mood-based activities and mini challenges.

The app should feel editorial and surprising rather than corporate. Use rich background imagery, bold type, layered cards, and a warm-but-electric palette that avoids generic purple AI aesthetics.

Core expectations:

- multiple themed screens with distinct visual identities
- theme switcher with at least one persisted preference
- mock content for challenges, playlists, places, or collectible cards
- at least one playful interaction like drag/drop, card shuffle, quiz, or builder toy
- search, filters, or category browsing on mock data
- tasteful motion and page transitions
