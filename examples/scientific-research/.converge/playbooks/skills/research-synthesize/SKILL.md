# Synthesis

Produce the final answer to the research question, citing the full evidence chain.

## Process

1. Read `evidence.json` and `analysis.md`
2. Construct a coherent answer to the original research question
3. Cite evidence for each major claim
4. Acknowledge limitations, inconclusive areas, and remaining questions
5. Provide confidence assessment

## Outputs

- `synthesis.md` — final research output:
  - Clear answer to the research question
  - Evidence chain for each claim
  - Confidence level (high/medium/low)
  - Limitations and caveats
  - Suggestions for further research

## Quality Criteria

- Directly addresses the research question
- Every major claim cites evidence from `evidence.json`
- Limitations are acknowledged
- Confidence is calibrated to the evidence quality
