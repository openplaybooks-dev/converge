# Academic Paper Section Drafting

Write a single section of an academic research paper.

## General Conventions

- Use formal academic tone throughout
- Cite sources using [Author, Year] notation
- Present quantitative findings with effect sizes and confidence intervals
- Use hedging language appropriately ("suggests", "indicates" vs "proves")
- Connect claims to evidence explicitly
- Maintain logical flow between paragraphs

## Section Types

Each section has specific requirements. Follow the section-specific guidelines provided in the task prompt.

### Abstract
- Single paragraph, 150-250 words
- Structure: background → method → results → conclusion

### Introduction
- Funnel structure: broad context → specific gap → research question
- End with paper structure overview

### Literature Review
- Organize thematically, not chronologically
- Compare and contrast, don't just list
- End with the specific gap this research addresses

### Methodology
- Reproducibility is the goal
- Document all statistical methods with formulas
- Acknowledge limitations upfront

### Results
- Present data objectively, no interpretation
- Use tables for quantitative summaries
- Report both significant and non-significant findings

### Discussion
- Interpret results in context of literature
- Address each hypothesis outcome
- Discuss practical implications

### Conclusion
- Brief, definitive — answer the question
- Acknowledge uncertainty
- Suggest future work

### References
- Consistent format throughout
- Every in-text citation must appear
- No references without in-text citations

## Quality Criteria

- Section meets minimum word count
- Academic tone maintained
- All claims cite evidence
- Logical flow between paragraphs
