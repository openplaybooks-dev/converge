# Idea

A retired lighthouse keeper on a remote Atlantic island discovers that the lighthouse lamp is a portal between dying worlds. Each night, refugees from vanishing realities arrive in the beam. When a child steps through and the lamp fails, he must relight it before dawn — or strand her forever in a world that doesn't want her.

## Genre & Tone

- Slow-burn cosmic drama with hopeful undertone
- Influences: *Arrival* (grounded sci-fi emotion), *The Lighthouse* (isolation, weather, grain), *A Ghost Story* (patience, stillness)
- Pace: contemplative — long takes, diegetic sound

## Target Runtime

20 minutes (short film). Adjust `vars.target_duration_minutes` in `playbook.yml` to scale.

## Non-goals

- No action set pieces
- No expository narration
- No on-screen text/titles in-clip (handled in NLE)
