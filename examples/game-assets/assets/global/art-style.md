# Art Style: 16-bit Retro

## Palette Constraints
- Limited to 16 colors per sprite
- Sharp, crisp pixel edges (no anti-aliasing)
- NES/SNES era color palette inspiration

## Color Guidelines
- Blue and silver armor (hero knight)
- Green and brown tones (forest elf)
- Dark purple and black with magical glow accents (shadow mage)
- Red accent colors for capes and highlights

## Resolution Standards
- Sprite frames: 128x128 pixels
- Sprite sheets: 4 frames per row, 2 rows max
- Backgrounds: 512x512 or 1024x1024 pixels

## Style Notes
- No gradients — use solid color fills
- Bold outlines (1-2 pixels)
- Distinct silhouettes for each character
- Consistent eye/face proportions within a character family
