# mage Class Style Guide

## Overview

The mage class embodies mystical power and arcane mastery. Characters in this class project an aura of otherworldly knowledge, with flowing robes and staff-based combat. The visual identity emphasizes magical energy, ethereal movement, and scholarly sophistication.

## Color Palette

**Primary Colors:**
- Deep Purple (#4A148C) - Arcane energy, mysticism
- Dark Blue (#1A237E) - Wisdom, depth
- Silver (#C0C0C0) - Magical essence, moonlight

**Secondary Colors:**
- Violet (#7B1FA2) - Spell effects, auras
- Midnight Blue (#0D47A1) - Shadows, night magic
- Cyan (#00BCD4) - Energy highlights, spell glows

**Accent Colors:**
- Gold (#FFD700) - Rune details, trim
- White (#FFFFFF) - Pure magical light

## Materials

**Armor Type:** Robes
- Flowing, layered fabric
- Lightweight, non-restrictive
- Ornate patterns and runes
- Hood or cowl optional

**Clothing:**
- Silk and velvet textures
- Long sleeves with wide cuffs
- Belts with pouches for reagents
- Decorative sashes

**Weapon Materials:**
- Staff: Polished wood or crystal
- Metal accents: Silver or gold
- Gemstone focus points
- Glowing runes or inscriptions

## Design Elements

**Common Visual Elements:**
- Flowing robes with multiple layers
- Arcane symbols and runes
- Staff as primary weapon
- Magical auras and particle effects
- Pointed or wide-brimmed hats (optional)
- Spell books or scrolls as accessories
- Glowing eyes or magical emanations

**Silhouette:**
- Vertical emphasis with long robes
- Staff extends silhouette upward
- Flowing fabric creates dynamic shapes
- Less bulky than warrior classes

## Animation Style

**Movement Characteristics:**
- Graceful, flowing motion
- Robes billow and sway
- Levitation or floating effects possible
- Staff used for balance and casting
- Minimal physical contact in combat

**Pose Style:**
- Upright, dignified posture
- Staff held vertically or at angle
- One hand often raised for casting
- Weight distributed evenly
- Mystical, contemplative stance

**Combat Style:**
- Ranged spell casting
- Staff used for channeling magic
- Defensive barriers and shields
- Area-of-effect spells
- Minimal melee engagement
