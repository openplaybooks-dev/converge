# Surface Gather

Collect high-level sources across identified topic areas.

## Process

1. **Retrieve content** for promising sources from rapid search
2. **Assess relevance** (high/medium/low) for each source
3. **Gather additional sources** per topic area (2-3 each)
4. **Maintain source variety** (papers, articles, reports, data)
5. **Update source registry** for deduplication tracking

## Source Types to Seek

- Research papers (peer-reviewed)
- High-quality web articles (established publications)
- Technical reports
- Data sources and statistics
- Expert opinions and analyses

## Quality Criteria

- At least 8 unique sources gathered
- Sources span multiple topic areas
- No exact duplicates (content hash check)
- Source registry updated with all new sources
