# Final Report

Generate the comprehensive final research report synthesizing all three layers.

## Report Structure

### 1. Executive Summary (150-250 words)
- 3-5 bullet points of most important findings
- Each bullet synthesizes across layers
- Overall confidence level stated

### 2. Key Findings by Layer
- **Layer 1**: Breadth survey discoveries with [SRC-N] citations
- **Layer 2**: Cross-area insights, connections, contradictions
- **Layer 3**: Definitive findings with verified reasoning

### 3. Deep Dive Analysis
- Comprehensive analysis of 1-2 most critical areas
- All supporting evidence with source citations
- Reasoning chains visible

### 4. Reasoning Chains
- For each major conclusion: the chain of evidence leading to it
- Contradiction resolution documented
- Gaps in reasoning where evidence was weak

### 5. Source Quality Overview
- Quality matrix: sources by reliability, recency, relevance
- Weak sources flagged with suggestions
- Key sources driving major conclusions identified

### 6. Limitations and Gaps
- Areas investigated but not resolved
- Contradictions that could not be resolved
- Evidence gaps requiring further research

### 7. References
- All sources gathered across all phases
- [SRC-N] IDs with full citation info
- Organized by layer/relevance

## Citation Format

- Inline citations: `[SRC-NNN]`
- Every factual claim must have citation
- Statistical claims cite source with data
- Comparative claims cite both sides

## Quality Criteria

- Every claim has [SRC-N] citation
- Reasoning chains visible and traceable
- Limitations honestly reported
- Report is comprehensive (3000+ words)
- Structure follows 7-section outline
