# Reasoning Chains

Trace how conclusions were derived, verify evidence quality, document the reasoning path.

## Process

1. **Identify major conclusions** from critical investigation
2. **Trace reasoning path** for each:
   - Evidence → Inference → Conclusion
   - Chain together multiple steps as applicable
3. **Identify assumptions** in the reasoning chain
4. **Assess chain strength** (high/medium/low) based on:
   - Strength of each link
   - Number of assumptions
   - Quality of underlying evidence
5. **Identify weak links** — where evidence is insufficient
6. **Document what would strengthen** the chain

## Chain Structure

Each reasoning chain includes:
- Final conclusion
- Step-by-step evidence and inference
- Assumptions made at each step
- Overall strength assessment
- Weakest link identification
- Strengthening recommendations

## Handling Unverifiable Claims

For claims that cannot be fully traced:
1. Document the claim and what makes it unverifiable
2. Note what evidence exists but is insufficient
3. Include in report as acknowledged limitation

## Quality Criteria

- All major conclusions have traceable reasoning chains
- Assumptions explicitly documented
- Weak links in chains identified
- Unverifiable claims honestly reported
