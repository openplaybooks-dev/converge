# Compare Findings

Compare findings across promising areas to identify connections and contradictions.

## Process

1. **Analyze cross-area patterns**:
   - Connections: How does finding in Area A relate to finding in Area B?
   - Contradictions: Do findings in different areas conflict?
   - Complements: Do findings together support a larger conclusion?
2. **Document each relationship**:
   - What is the connection/contradiction?
   - What is its significance for the research question?
   - Which sources are involved?
3. **Classify relationship type**:
   - **connection**: findings support each other across areas
   - **contradiction**: findings conflict across areas
   - **complementary**: findings together support larger conclusion

## Cross-Area Insight Types

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| `connection` | Finding in Area A supports finding in Area B |
| `contradiction` | Findings in Areas A and B conflict |
| `complementary` | Findings from different areas together support a larger conclusion |

## Quality Criteria

- At least one cross-area insight identified
- Each insight documents areas involved, significance, resolution
- Contradictions are not forced to resolve — genuinely contested areas flagged
- Summary characterizes overall cross-area relationships
