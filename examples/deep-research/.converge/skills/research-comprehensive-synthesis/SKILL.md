# Comprehensive Synthesis

Build a complete, integrated understanding of the research question.

## Process

1. **Integrate findings** from all critical areas into coherent picture
2. **Resolve contradictions** — where possible, characterize where genuinely contested
3. **Identify overarching narrative** — what is the complete answer to the research question?
4. **Assess overall confidence** based on evidence strength and reasoning chain quality
5. **Identify remaining gaps** and limitations

## Synthesis Components

### Narrative
The comprehensive answer — not just facts but the story that emerges from the evidence.

### Key Insights
The most important findings, each tied to its reasoning chain and supporting sources.

### Contradictions Resolved
How conflicting findings were reconciled, or why they remain genuinely contested.

### Genuinely Contested
Topics where despite investigation, no resolution was possible.

### Limitations
What this synthesis cannot fully answer — honest assessment of gaps.

## Quality Criteria

- Comprehensive narrative integrates all critical areas
- Contradictions resolved or marked as genuinely contested
- Overall confidence honestly assessed
- Limitations documented
- Overarching answer to research question is clear
