# Critical Investigation

Deep investigation of the most critical areas from Layer 2.

## Process

1. **Read prior aggregation** — identify critical areas and contradiction triggers
2. **Execute deep searches** — strengthen evidence base for each critical area
3. **Find corroborating sources** — additional evidence for central conclusions
4. **Address contradictions** — investigate and resolve contradiction triggers from prior layer
5. **Build evidence map** — comprehensive mapping of strong, moderate, and weak evidence

## Evidence Map Structure

For each critical area, document:
- **Strongest evidence**: Claims with multiple high-quality source support
- **Moderate evidence**: Claims with some support but need strengthening
- **Weak evidence**: Claims with insufficient support or low-quality sources

## Contradiction Resolution

For each contradiction identified in prior layers:
1. Investigate the sources on both sides
2. Understand why they disagree (methodology, scope, data, interpretation)
3. Determine if resolution is possible or if genuinely contested
4. Document resolution or characterization as genuinely contested

## Quality Criteria

- Each critical area has comprehensive evidence map
- Contradiction triggers from prior layer are addressed
- Conclusions grounded in specific sources
- Confidence level assessed per area
