# Area Identification

Identify 3-5 promising areas from gathered sources that warrant deeper investigation.

## Process

1. **Review all sources** and their content summaries
2. **Identify thematic clusters** — areas where multiple sources converge
3. **Score each area** on multiple dimensions:
   - Evidence strength: How many sources support this?
   - Insight potential: Will deeper investigation yield new understanding?
   - Novelty: Is this well-explored or does it have gaps?
   - Relevance: How directly does it address the research question?
4. **Compute composite**: `0.3*evidence + 0.3*insight + 0.2*novelty + 0.2*relevance`
5. **Rank and select** top 3-5 promising areas

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Evidence Strength | 30% | Number and quality of supporting sources |
| Insight Potential | 30% | Likelihood of new understanding from deeper dive |
| Novelty | 20% | How unexplored this area is |
| Relevance | 20% | Directness to the research question |

## Quality Criteria

- At least 3 promising areas identified
- Each area has explicit scoring on all dimensions
- Rejected areas documented with rationale
- Areas are distinct (non-overlapping) or overlap is noted
