# Cross Analysis

Analyze cross-source patterns within each promising area.

## Process

1. **Identify patterns** across sources per area:
   - Converging evidence: multiple sources support same claim
   - Diverging claims: sources disagree on facts or interpretations
2. **Trace claim provenance** — which sources support which claims
3. **Analyze disagreement** — why do sources diverge?
   - Statistical discrepancy
   - Interpretation difference
   - Quality difference
   - Different scope/context
4. **Document reasoning patterns** — how conclusions are derived from evidence

## Converging vs Diverging Claims

**Converging claims** (multiple sources agree):
- High confidence in the claim
- Can combine evidence from multiple sources

**Diverging claims** (sources disagree):
- Requires deeper analysis to understand why
- May indicate genuinely contested territory
- Must not force resolution when evidence is ambiguous

## Quality Criteria

- All areas have cross-source analysis
- Converging and diverging claims documented
- Reasoning patterns trace evidence to conclusions
- Nature of disagreement is characterized
