# Frontier Research Report

Produce the final research report after convergence.

## Process

1. Read `research-state.json` for the complete knowledge model
2. Read `research-ledger.jsonl` for the full epoch trajectory
3. Read final epoch's `selection.json` for the latest merged insights
4. Synthesize findings into a structured report

## Report Structure

1. **Research Question** — restated with context
2. **Executive Summary** — key findings in 3-5 bullet points
3. **Knowledge Map** — all established claims with confidence levels
4. **Insight Trajectory** — how understanding evolved across epochs (delta curve)
5. **Key Connections** — relationships between claims that reveal deeper structure
6. **Dead Ends** — what was explored and failed (negative results)
7. **Open Questions** — remaining frontier edges not fully explored
8. **Methodology Notes** — which beam approaches were most productive

## Quality Criteria

- Report is self-contained (readable without other files)
- Claims are attributed to specific epochs and beams
- Confidence levels are honest (not everything is "high confidence")
- Dead ends are documented as valuable negative results
- Open questions point to genuine unknowns (not just "more research needed")
