# Folio - Novel Reader

## Purpose

Folio is a mobile-first application for browsing, reading, and listening to serialized web novels. It brings the joy of discovering new stories together with a distraction-free reading experience optimized for long-form content on mobile devices.

## Core Features

- **Home Feed** — Personalized feed with "Continue Reading" hero card, trending stories, and curated collections
- **Browse & Discovery** — Genre-based browsing with filters, search, and recommendation cards
- **Novel Detail** — Book page with synopsis, chapter list, ratings, and related novels
- **Chapter Reader** — Distraction-free reading with customizable fonts, themes, and progress tracking
- **Audio Player** — Text-to-speech with mini-player and full-screen audio controls
- **Personal Library** — Tabbed bookshelf (Reading, Completed, Wishlist) with progress bars
- **Profile & Stats** — Reading statistics, achievements, and preferences

## Target Audience

- **Night Readers** — Read before bed, prefer dark themes and warm tones
- **Series Trackers** — Follow multiple ongoing series, need progress tracking
- **Commuter Listeners** — Listen to chapters during commute via audio playback

## Visual Direction

- Dark-first design with warm paper-like undertones
- Liquid glass surfaces with subtle depth and floating UI elements
- Spring-based micro-animations for tactile feedback
- Adaptive tinting — cover art colors bleed into UI accents
- Typography-forward — large, readable serif for content, clean sans-serif for UI

## Technical Requirements

- Flutter with Material 3 theming
- Offline-capable with local storage
- Responsive layout (phone-first, tablet-adaptive)
- Smooth 60fps scrolling and page transitions
- Accessible (screen reader, dynamic type, reduced motion)
