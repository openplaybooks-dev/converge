# Asset Specification: week-20

## Overview
Baby size comparison illustration showing a banana — representing the fetus at week 20 of pregnancy. The illustration is used on the HomeScreen inside the HeroIllustrationCard widget.

## Visual Description
A single, friendly banana rendered in a soft, curved style. The body is a gentle crescent arc, widest at the center and tapering toward both ends. The peel surface is smooth with two faint longitudinal ridges running along the outer curve. A small dark-brown stem nub sits at the top end, and the blossom tip at the bottom tapers to a soft rounded point. The banana surface uses a warm coral-tinted yellow gradient, lighter on the inner-facing belly to suggest a highlight, deepening toward the outer curve and edges. A thin, tapered shadow sits beneath the banana. A faint circular halo sits behind the banana for scale context. The overall feel is warm, minimal, and approachable.

## Color Palette
- Primary (banana body): #F28B8B (soft coral)
- Accent (stem/tip): #8B7ED8 (soft lilac)
- Halo/glow: #FDEEEE (blush pink)
- Background: transparent

## Dimensions
- ViewBox: 0 0 200 200
- Padding: 10% (content within 20-180 range)

## Style Notes
- Stroke width: 1.5px
- Corner radius: 8px on rectangular elements
- Flat design with subtle gradient fills for depth
- Rounded, organic shapes — no sharp edges
- Mood: friendly, reassuring

## Variants
- Light mode: colors as specified above
- Dark mode: swap halo #FDEEEE to #2A2240, keep banana colors at full saturation
