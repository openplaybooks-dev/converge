# Asset Specification: week-16

## Overview
Baby size comparison illustration showing an avocado — representing the fetus at week 16 of pregnancy. The illustration is used on the HomeScreen inside the HeroIllustrationCard widget.

## Visual Description
A single, friendly avocado rendered in a soft, rounded style. The avocado has an elongated pear shape — wider at the bottom, tapering gently toward the top. The outer skin is a rich green with subtle texture dots suggesting the characteristic bumpy surface. A vertical cross-section cutaway on the front reveals the pale yellow-green flesh interior and a large round pit in the lower-center. The pit is a warm brown circle with a subtle lighter highlight. A small shadow sits at the base. A faint circular halo sits behind the avocado for scale context. The overall feel is warm, minimal, and approachable.

## Color Palette
- Primary (avocado skin): #FF6B6B (coral)
- Accent (pit/details): #9B59B6 (lilac)
- Halo/glow: #FDEEEE (blush pink)
- Background: transparent

## Dimensions
- ViewBox: 0 0 200 200
- Padding: 10% (content within 20-180 range)

## Style Notes
- Stroke width: 1.5px
- Corner radius: 8px on rectangular elements
- Flat design with subtle gradient fills for depth
- Rounded, organic shapes — no sharp edges
- Mood: friendly, reassuring

## Variants
- Light mode: colors as specified above
- Dark mode: swap halo #FDEEEE to #2A2240, keep avocado colors at full saturation
