# Asset Specification: week-06

## Overview
Baby size comparison illustration showing a sweet pea — representing the embryo at week 6 of pregnancy. The illustration is used on the HomeScreen inside the HeroIllustrationCard widget.

## Visual Description
A single, friendly sweet pea pod rendered in a soft, rounded style. The pod is a gently curved crescent shape with two small round peas peeking out from a slight opening at the top. The pod has a smooth surface with a subtle central vein line running along its length. A tiny curling tendril extends from the stem end. The pod is centered in the viewbox with a faint circular halo behind it for scale context. The overall feel is warm, minimal, and approachable.

## Color Palette
- Primary (pod body): #FF6B6B (coral)
- Accent (peas/tendril detail): #9B59B6 (lilac)
- Halo/glow: #FDEEEE (blush pink)
- Background: transparent

## Dimensions
- ViewBox: 0 0 200 200
- Padding: 10% (content within 20-180 range)

## Style Notes
- Stroke width: 1.5px
- Corner radius: 8px on rectangular elements
- Flat design with subtle gradient fills for depth
- Rounded, organic shapes — no sharp edges
- Mood: friendly, reassuring

## Variants
- Light mode: colors as specified above
- Dark mode: swap halo #FDEEEE to #2A2240, keep pod colors at full saturation
