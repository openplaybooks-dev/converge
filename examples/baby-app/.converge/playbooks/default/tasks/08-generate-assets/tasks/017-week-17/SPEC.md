# Asset Specification: week-17

## Overview
Baby size comparison illustration showing a turnip — representing the fetus at week 17 of pregnancy. The illustration is used on the HomeScreen inside the HeroIllustrationCard widget.

## Visual Description
A single, friendly turnip rendered in a soft, rounded style. The bulb is a squat globe shape — wide at the middle, tapering to a small root tail at the bottom and a flat crown at the top. The lower two-thirds of the bulb are creamy white, transitioning through a gentle gradient into a rich purple-magenta shoulder at the top. Two or three short, rounded leaves sprout upward from the crown in a lively fan shape, colored in a fresh green with lighter vein lines. A thin, tapered root extends from the base. A small shadow sits beneath the turnip. A faint circular halo sits behind the turnip for scale context. The overall feel is warm, minimal, and approachable.
