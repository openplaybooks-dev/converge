# Asset Specification: week-18

## Overview
Baby size comparison illustration showing a bell pepper — representing the fetus at week 18 of pregnancy. The illustration is used on the HomeScreen inside the HeroIllustrationCard widget.

## Visual Description
A single, friendly bell pepper rendered in a soft, rounded style. The body is a wide, gently lobed dome shape — broad at the shoulders and tapering to a rounded base with three or four subtle vertical lobes. A short, curved stem sits at the top, slightly off-center, with a small calyx of rounded sepals fanning outward. The pepper surface uses a warm coral gradient, lighter on the front-facing lobe to suggest a gentle highlight, deepening toward the edges. A few soft creases run vertically between the lobes. A thin, tapered shadow sits beneath the pepper. A faint circular halo sits behind the pepper for scale context. The overall feel is warm, minimal, and approachable.

## Color Palette

- Accent (stem/calyx): #9B59B6 (lilac)
- Halo/glow: #FDEEEE (blush pink)
- Background: transparent
