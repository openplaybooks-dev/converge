# Asset Specification: week-21

## Overview
Baby size comparison illustration showing a carrot — representing the fetus at week 21 of pregnancy. The illustration is used on the HomeScreen inside the HeroIllustrationCard widget.

## Visual Description
A single, friendly carrot rendered in a soft, rounded style. The body is a tapered cone shape, widest at the top and narrowing to a gentle rounded point at the bottom. The surface is smooth with two or three faint horizontal ridges suggesting natural segments. A small tuft of feathery green leaves fans outward from the top, with three to four short fronds curving gently outward. The carrot body uses a warm coral gradient, lighter along the center to suggest a highlight, deepening toward the edges and tip. A thin, tapered shadow sits beneath the carrot. A faint circular halo sits behind the carrot for scale context. The overall feel is warm, minimal, and approachable.

## Color Palette
- Primary (carrot body): #F28B8B (soft coral)
- Accent (leaves/top): #8B7ED8 (soft lilac)
- Halo/glow: #FDEEEE (blush pink)
- Background: transparent
