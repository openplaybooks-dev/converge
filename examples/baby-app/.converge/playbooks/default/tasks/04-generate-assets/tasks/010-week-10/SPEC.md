# Asset Specification: week-10

## Overview
Baby size comparison illustration showing a prune — representing the fetus at week 10 of pregnancy. The illustration is used on the HomeScreen inside the HeroIllustrationCard widget.

## Visual Description
A single, friendly prune rendered in a soft, rounded style. The prune has a plump, slightly wrinkled oval body with a gentle dimple running vertically down the center. A small curved stem protrudes from the top. Fine, shallow creases radiate outward from the dimple to suggest the dried-fruit texture without appearing overly detailed. A subtle highlight appears on the upper-left surface to suggest gentle lighting. A faint circular halo sits behind the prune for scale context. The overall feel is warm, minimal, and approachable.

## Color Palette
- Primary (prune body): #FF6B6B (coral)
- Accent (stem/creases): #9B59B6 (lilac)
- Halo/glow: #FDEEEE (blush pink)
- Background: transparent

## Dimensions
- ViewBox: 0 0 200 200
- Padding: 10% (content within 20-180 range)

## Style Notes
- Stroke width: 1.5px
- Corner radius: 8px on rectangular elements
- Flat design with subtle gradient fills for depth
- Rounded, organic shapes — no sharp edges
- Mood: friendly, reassuring

## Variants
- Light mode: colors as specified above
- Dark mode: swap halo #FDEEEE to #2A2240, keep prune colors at full saturation
