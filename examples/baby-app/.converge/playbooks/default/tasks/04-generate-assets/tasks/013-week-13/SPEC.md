# Asset Specification: week-13

## Overview
Baby size comparison illustration showing a peach — representing the fetus at week 13 of pregnancy. The illustration is used on the HomeScreen inside the HeroIllustrationCard widget.

## Visual Description
A single, friendly peach rendered in a soft, rounded style. The peach has a plump, slightly oval body with a subtle vertical crease running from top to bottom. A small curved stem protrudes from the top, accompanied by two narrow pointed leaves. A gentle highlight appears on the upper-left surface to suggest soft lighting. A light fuzzy texture is implied through subtle tonal variation on the skin. A faint circular halo sits behind the peach for scale context. The overall feel is warm, minimal, and approachable.

## Color Palette
- Primary (peach body): #FF6B6B (coral)
- Accent (stem/leaves/crease): #9B59B6 (lilac)
- Halo/glow: #FDEEEE (blush pink)
- Background: transparent

## Dimensions
- ViewBox: 0 0 200 200
- Padding: 10% (content within 20-180 range)

## Style Notes
- Stroke width: 1.5px
- Corner radius: 8px on rectangular elements
- Flat design with subtle gradient fills for depth
- Rounded, organic shapes — no sharp edges
- Mood: friendly, reassuring

## Variants
- Light mode: colors as specified above
- Dark mode: swap halo #FDEEEE to #2A2240, keep peach colors at full saturation
