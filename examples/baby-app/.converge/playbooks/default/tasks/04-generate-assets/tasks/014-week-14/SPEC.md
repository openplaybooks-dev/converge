# Asset Specification: week-14

## Overview
Baby size comparison illustration showing a lemon — representing the fetus at week 14 of pregnancy. The illustration is used on the HomeScreen inside the HeroIllustrationCard widget.

## Visual Description
A single, friendly lemon rendered in a soft, rounded style. The lemon has an elongated oval body with tapered ends, giving it the characteristic citrus shape. A small stubby stem sits at one end, with a single small rounded leaf. Fine dimpled texture is implied across the skin through subtle tonal dots. A gentle highlight appears on the upper-left surface to suggest soft lighting. A faint circular halo sits behind the lemon for scale context. The overall feel is warm, minimal, and approachable.

## Color Palette
- Primary (lemon body): #FF6B6B (coral)
- Accent (stem/leaf/dimples): #9B59B6 (lilac)
- Halo/glow: #FDEEEE (blush pink)
- Background: transparent

## Dimensions
- ViewBox: 0 0 200 200
- Padding: 10% (content within 20-180 range)

## Style Notes
- Stroke width: 1.5px
- Corner radius: 8px on rectangular elements
- Flat design with subtle gradient fills for depth
- Rounded, organic shapes — no sharp edges
- Mood: friendly, reassuring

## Variants
- Light mode: colors as specified above
- Dark mode: swap halo #FDEEEE to #2A2240, keep lemon colors at full saturation
