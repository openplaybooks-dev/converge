# Asset Specification: week-08

## Overview
Baby size comparison illustration showing a raspberry — representing the embryo at week 8 of pregnancy. The illustration is used on the HomeScreen inside the HeroIllustrationCard widget.

## Visual Description
A single, friendly raspberry rendered in a soft, rounded style. The berry is composed of a cluster of small, plump drupelets arranged in an oval dome shape. A short stem with two small serrated leaves sits at the top. A subtle highlight appears on the upper-left drupelets to suggest gentle lighting. A faint circular halo sits behind the berry for scale context. The overall feel is warm, minimal, and approachable.

## Color Palette
- Primary (drupelets): #FF6B6B (coral)
- Accent (stem/leaves): #9B59B6 (lilac)
- Halo/glow: #FDEEEE (blush pink)
- Background: transparent

## Dimensions
- ViewBox: 0 0 200 200
- Padding: 10% (content within 20-180 range)

## Style Notes
- Stroke width: 1.5px
- Corner radius: 8px on rectangular elements
- Flat design with subtle gradient fills for depth
- Rounded, organic shapes — no sharp edges
- Mood: friendly, reassuring

## Variants
- Light mode: colors as specified above
- Dark mode: swap halo #FDEEEE to #2A2240, keep berry colors at full saturation
