# Asset Specification: week-19

## Overview
Baby size comparison illustration showing a tomato — representing the fetus at week 19 of pregnancy. The illustration is used on the HomeScreen inside the HeroIllustrationCard widget.

## Visual Description
A single, friendly tomato rendered in a soft, rounded style. The body is a wide, slightly flattened sphere with gentle vertical ridges suggesting the classic segmented tomato shape. A small star-shaped calyx of five pointed sepals sits at the top, with a short curved stem rising from the center. The tomato surface uses a warm coral gradient, lighter on the upper-front face to suggest a gentle highlight, deepening toward the bottom and edges. Subtle creases radiate outward from the stem area between segments. A thin, tapered shadow sits beneath the tomato. A faint circular halo sits behind the tomato for scale context. The overall feel is warm, minimal, and approachable.

## Color Palette
- Primary (tomato body): #F28B8B (soft coral)
- Accent (stem/calyx): #8B7ED8 (soft lilac)
- Halo/glow: #FDEEEE (blush pink)
- Background: transparent

## Dimensions
- ViewBox: 0 0 200 200
- Padding: 10% (content within 20-180 range)

## Style Notes
- Stroke width: 1.5px
- Corner radius: 8px on rectangular elements
- Flat design with subtle gradient fills for depth
- Rounded, organic shapes — no sharp edges
- Mood: friendly, reassuring

## Variants
- Light mode: colors as specified above
