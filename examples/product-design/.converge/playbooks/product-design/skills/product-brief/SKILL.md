# Product Brief Skill

Write a product brief document.

## Sections

- **Problem**: User pain point or market opportunity
- **Solution**: How this product addresses it
- **Target Users**: Primary and secondary segments
- **Success Metrics**: Measurable outcomes
- **Core Value Proposition**: One-sentence why users choose this
