# Persona Development Skill

Create user persona documents.

## Persona Sections

- **Role**: Job title and context
- **Goals**: What they want to accomplish
- **Pain Points**: Frustrations and workarounds
- **Behaviors**: How they interact with similar products
- **Tech Proficiency**: Comfort level with tools
- **Quote**: Representative statement
