About

I design products that respect both the people using them and the constraints teams operate under. Over the past eight years I've worked with early-stage startups and established companies — from zero-to-one features to large-scale redesigns of existing products.

My approach is grounded in user research and refined through iteration. I care about the details — spacing, typography, motion — because I believe those details are where trust is built or lost. I work closely with engineering to ensure what ships matches what was designed.

Previously at Stripe, Notion, and several early-stage companies. Currently available for freelance projects.
