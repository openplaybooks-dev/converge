About

The designer behind the work

I'm a brand and digital designer based in San Francisco, working with studios, startups, and independent creators who want work that feels intentional — not templated. My practice sits at the intersection of strategic thinking and craft: getting to the right answer, then making it look effortless.

Before going independent, I spent six years at a boutique branding agency in New York, where I learned that the best design is often invisible. Clients don't remember the process — they remember how the work made them feel, and how it performed.

When I'm not designing, I'm hiking the Marin Headlands, collecting type specimens, and trying to figure out what makes some brands feel inevitable.
