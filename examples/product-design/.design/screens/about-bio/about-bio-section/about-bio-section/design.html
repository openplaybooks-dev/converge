Maya Chen

Brand and digital designer crafting quiet, confident identities

I've spent the last eight years helping studios, startups, and independent creators find their visual voice. My work lives at the intersection of strategy and craft — I believe a great brand system isn't just beautiful, it's coherent across every touchpoint and meaningful to the people who use it.

Before going independent, I led design at two agencies in San Francisco, where I learned that the best outcomes come from deep collaboration with clients who care as much about the process as the result. I take on a small number of projects each year so I can give each one the attention it deserves.

When I'm not designing, you'll find me hiking trails in Marin County, collecting type specimens, or helping local restaurants with their brand identities at reduced rates.

Process note

How I work

Every project starts with a discovery conversation — I need to understand not just what you want to look like, but what you're trying to say and who you're trying to reach. From there, I move through research, concept exploration, and refinement, sharing work at key milestones so we stay aligned. I don't use templates or AI-generated assets — every solution is built from scratch for the specific context.
