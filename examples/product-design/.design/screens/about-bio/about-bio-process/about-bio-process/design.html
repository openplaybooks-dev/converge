Process

How we work together

Every project follows a clear arc — from understanding your situation to delivering something built to last.

  1. 1

    Discovery & Alignment

    We start with an open conversation about your business, your audience, and what success looks like. I ask uncomfortable questions early so we don't find them mid-project. You'll leave this call with clarity, regardless of whether we work together.

    1–2 sessions · 1 week
  2. 2

    Strategy & Direction

    Before any visual work, I document the strategic foundation: positioning, personality, and the rules that will guide every decision. This is the document your team will reference long after I'm gone.

    5–7 business days
  3. 3

    Design & Iteration

    I present one direction at a time — fully resolved, never half-baked. You give feedback, I evolve. Three rounds is typical; four is the cap. Beyond that, we've lost the thread and we go back to strategy.

    2–4 weeks depending on scope
  4. 4

    Handoff & Enablement

    Production-ready files, brand guidelines, and a 30-minute walk-through so your team can run with it confidently. I don't disappear after delivery — questions welcome for 30 days post-handoff.

    3–5 business days

Ready to start a conversation? Email me — I respond within 1–2 business days.
