# Product Brief: Portfolio Page

## Concept & Vision

A single-page personal portfolio site for a freelance designer. The site serves as a digital storefront — minimal, refined, and focused entirely on showcasing work samples and contact information. The personality is quiet confidence: letting the work speak while the design stays out of the way.

---

## Product Overview

- **Type**: Personal portfolio / single-page marketing site
- **Core function**: Display design work and make it easy for potential clients to get in touch
- **Audience**: Prospective clients reviewing a freelance designer's work
- **Platform**: Web (responsive, desktop-first)

---

## User Stories

| # | Who | Wants | So they can |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Client browsing on desktop | Browse work samples quickly | Evaluate quality and style in under 60 seconds |
| 2 | Client browsing on mobile | View work on their phone | Contact the designer on the go |
| 3 | Interested client | Find contact information immediately | Reach out without hunting |
| 4 | Returning client | Contact the designer directly | Send a project inquiry |

---

## Core Features

1. **Hero section** — Name, tagline, and brief positioning statement above the fold
2. **Work showcase** — Grid or list of design work samples with thumbnails and titles
3. **About / bio section** — Short description of the designer's background and approach
4. **Contact section** — Email address and/or contact form; clear call-to-action
5. **Responsive layout** — Works cleanly on desktop (1024px+) and mobile (375px+)

---

## Design Direction

- **Aesthetic**: Clean, minimal, editorial — white space is a feature, not a bug
- **Typography**: Clear hierarchy; primary typeface supports both display and body
- **Color**: Restrained palette; work samples are the color
- **Layout**: Single column on mobile, grid-based on desktop; no scrolling gimmicks

---

## Out of Scope (v1)

- Blog or articles
- E-commerce or job board
- Multi-page navigation
- CMS or dynamic content management
- Analytics or tracking scripts

---

## Quality Criteria

- First load under 2s on a fast connection
- No layout shift after initial paint
- All images have alt text (for portfolio pieces)
- Contact section is accessible (readable contrast, tappable targets on mobile)
- Works in latest Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge
