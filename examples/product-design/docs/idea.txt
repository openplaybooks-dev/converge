Product Idea: MentorMatch — A platform connecting junior developers with experienced mentors

Problem: Junior developers struggle to find personalized guidance from experienced professionals who understand their specific tech stack, career goals, and learning style. Traditional mentoring programs are often too structured or don't match well.

Solution: An AI-assisted mentor matching platform where junior developers can find mentors based on:
- Tech stack compatibility
- Career path alignment
- Communication style preferences
- Availability and time zones
- Mentoring goals (job prep, skill building, career transition)

Target Users:
1. Junior developers (0-3 years experience) seeking guidance
2. Mid-level developers (3-5 years) wanting to mentor and grow leadership skills
3. Senior developers (5+ years) looking to give back and build their network

Key Features Needed:
- Mentor matching algorithm
- Scheduling and availability management
- Session tracking and progress metrics
- Feedback and review system
- Resource sharing and learning paths
- Community forums and group mentoring

Success Metrics:
- Match satisfaction score > 4.5/5
- Session completion rate > 85%
- User retention > 70% after 3 months
- Mentor-mentee relationship duration > 6 months

Platform: Web-first, mobile-responsive
Timeline: MVP in 3 months, full launch in 6 months
